# New Project Interview

Ask focused questions in no more than three rounds. Ask two or three questions per round and summarize understanding between rounds.

## Round 1: Purpose

- What is being created, for whom, and what problem or desire does it address?
- What are the one to three primary outcomes?
- What is explicitly outside the current direction?

## Round 2: Experience

- How should the project feel? Ask for three to five concrete qualities.
- What should it never feel like? Offer contrasts where useful.
- Which products, sites, designs, or projects are references? Preserve URLs exactly and ask what to borrow or avoid from each.

## Round 3: Execution constraints

- Which platforms, surfaces, languages, accessibility requirements, or brand assets matter?
- Is a visual frontend in scope? If yes, which design-skill family or style is preferred? Do not select multiple primary visual skills.
- What decisions are non-negotiable and which remain exploratory?
- Must any selected nonstandard skill family be available to other contributors? Ask only after design and workflow direction is clear; do not request an inventory of every installed skill.

If the user cannot answer, record the item under Open Questions. Do not replace missing answers with generic product language.

After writing the draft, show the Core Contract and ask for explicit confirmation before marking it confirmed.
